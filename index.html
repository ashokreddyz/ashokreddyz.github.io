// instant_pot_batch_recipes — sunday cook · 5 portions each
Chicken / Mutton Palao
MEAT · 5 PORTIONS
520kcal
42gprotein
58gcarbs
8gfat
Ingredients
  • Chicken breast / mutton (curry cut)1 kg
  • Basmati rice (soaked 20 min)400g
  • Onion sliced3 large
  • Tomato chopped2
  • Ginger garlic paste2 tbsp
  • Palao / biryani masala2 tbsp
  • Whole spices (bay, clove, cardamom, cinnamon)1 set
  • Yogurt (curd)3 tbsp
  • Oil or ghee1 tbsp
  • Water600 ml
  • Coriander + mint, turmeric, saltto taste
Steps — Instant Pot
1
Sauté High: ghee → cumin + whole spices → sliced onions golden (~8 min) → ginger garlic paste 2 min 10 min
2
Add meat, sear all sides. Add tomatoes, turmeric, masala, salt, curd. Mix well to coat meat. 5 min
3
Layer drained rice over meat — DO NOT stir. Pour water evenly. Scatter mint + coriander on top.
4
Seal lid → Pressure Cook High: 12 min chicken / 22 min mutton → natural release 10 min → quick release 22–32 min
5
Fluff gently from edges. Divide into 5 airtight boxes. Cool before fridge. Good 4–5 days.
Dal Khichdi
VEG · 5 PORTIONS
420kcal
18gprotein
72gcarbs
9gfiber
Ingredients
  • Moong dal or toor dal (washed)200g
  • Basmati or brown rice (washed)300g
  • Onion chopped2
  • Tomato chopped2
  • Ginger garlic paste1½ tbsp
  • Cumin seeds1 tsp
  • Turmeric, coriander powder, garam masala½ tsp each
  • Ghee1 tbsp
  • Water900 ml
  • Lemon juice, saltto taste
Steps — Instant Pot / Stovetop Pressure Cooker
1
Sauté: ghee → cumin splutters → onions translucent (~5 min) → ginger garlic paste 1 min 6 min
2
Add tomatoes, turmeric, coriander, garam masala, salt. Cook until oil separates (~4 min). 4 min
3
Add washed dal + rice. Stir to coat masala. Pour water. Mix once.
4
Seal lid → Pressure Cook High 10 min → natural release 10 min → quick release 20 min
5
Stir, squeeze lemon, adjust thickness with hot water. Divide into 5 boxes. Serve alongside pan-fried tilapia fillet for full protein hit. Add 1 boiled egg on top as a bonus topper.

Spiced Tilapia Fillets
FISH · DAILY · 10 MIN
180kcal
36gprotein
0gcarbs
4gfat
Ingredients (1 serving = 1 fillet ~170g)
  • Tilapia fillet (fresh or frozen-thawed)170g
  • Turmeric¼ tsp
  • Red chilli powder or paprika½ tsp
  • Coriander powder½ tsp
  • Ginger garlic paste½ tsp
  • Lemon juice1 tsp
  • Saltto taste
  • Oil spray (or ½ tsp oil)minimal
Steps — Pan or Air Fryer
1
Pat fillet dry. Mix turmeric, chilli, coriander, ginger garlic paste, lemon, salt into a paste. Rub all over fillet both sides.
2
Pan: heat non-stick on medium-high, spray oil, cook 3–4 min per side until golden and flaky. Air fryer: 200°C for 8–10 min, flip halfway. 8–10 min
3
Serve alongside Dal Khichdi or Palao. Squeeze fresh lemon on top. Done — 36g protein in 10 minutes.
💡
Batch option: Marinate 4–5 fillets Sunday, store raw in fridge. Pan-fry fresh each night in 10 min — much better texture than reheating cooked fish.

// supplement_stack — minimal · science-backed · no wasted money
⚡
Creatine Monohydrate ✓ TAKING
5g daily
Most researched supplement in existence. Increases ATP production → more reps, more strength, faster recovery. Non-negotiable on PPL 6-day.
⏱ Mix in water with morning oats or post-gym. Timing irrelevant — daily consistency is everything.
🥛
Whey Protein
2 scoops/day (25g each = 50g total)
Fast-digesting complete protein. Covers 50g of 158g daily target without extra calories. Non-negotiable during a cut when food volume is reduced.
⏱ Scoop 1: morning in oats. Scoop 2: immediately post-gym before driving home.
☀️
Vitamin D3
2,000–4,000 IU daily
~80% of South Asians in the US are deficient. Low D3 directly lowers testosterone and recovery speed — affects gym performance and cognitive focus for study sessions.
⏱ Morning with breakfast (fat-soluble — absorbs with ghee/almonds).
🌙
Magnesium Glycinate
300mg nightly
Depleted by heavy training. Improves deep sleep quality (critical for muscle repair and OSCP memory consolidation). Reduces cortisol — new job + certs + 6-day gym is high load.
⏱ Night — 30 min before sleep. Glycinate form: no laxative effect unlike oxide form.
🐟
Omega-3 Fish Oil
1–2g EPA/DHA daily
Reduces workout inflammation and joint soreness (important on 6-day PPL). Supports brain focus for long OSCP sessions. Keep refrigerated to avoid oxidation.
⏱ With dinner.
🚫
Skip These (for now)
Save money
Pre-workout → black coffee does it. BCAAs → redundant with 2 scoops whey + meat diet. Fat burners → useless, deficit does the work. Multivitamin → D3 + omega-3 + real food covers it.
Monthly cost: Creatine ~$15 · Whey 2kg ~$45 · D3 ~$8 · Magnesium glycinate ~$12 · Fish oil ~$15 = ~$95/month total. Everything else is food.
